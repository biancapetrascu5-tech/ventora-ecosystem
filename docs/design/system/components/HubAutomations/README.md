# HubAutomations

Hub · Afacerea · "Automatizări" (D4-23, D4-24): the business sees which automations it has, with the last sending, and the UsageMeter of automated messages; it cannot turn them on or off — only Founder does, with audit. Keys `automations.*` in `i18n/automations.json`, 6 languages.

**The page.** The UsageMeter "Mesaje automate în noiembrie" (only messages sent count), the Notice "Pornirea și oprirea le face echipa Ventora Systems. Scrie-ne dacă vrei o schimbare." with "Cere o schimbare" (a Support request), and the 6 automations: name, "Pornită" / "Oprită", what it does, the last sending; an automation that is off has "Cere pornirea". Settings that belong to the business stay editable here: the weeks for reactivation, the birthday discount. A line says the reminder before an appointment is in EasyBook · Setări · Reamintiri.

**Who.** Owner and admins. Members see that the page is theirs only ("Automatizările le văd proprietarul și adminii."). Empty: "Nicio automatizare pornită".

**Decided (S116, 4 Nov. 2026).** S116-D22 · The business sets the birthday discount and the reactivation weeks itself, here, without Founder.
