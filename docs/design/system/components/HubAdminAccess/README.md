# HubAdminAccess

Business administration — roles and app access: a role × app matrix on desktop and tablet; on phones you pick a role and switch apps one by one. Apps outside the plan or temporarily unavailable carry an AccessBadge and cannot be granted.

Shown at 1280 (desktop), 820 (tablet portrait) and 390 (phone), in light and dark, built only from the system's components.

After the product review: every change shows a Toast with "Anulează" ("Am dat acces la TeamSpace pentru Recepție."); "Vezi ca {rol}" previews the launcher of a role; the grid can be walked with the arrow keys.
