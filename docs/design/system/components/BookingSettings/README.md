# BookingSettings

Booking — app settings: opening hours per day (toggle + time range), booking rules (notice, free cancellation, buffer, confirmation mode), automatic reminders with their SMS usage and the owner's "Cumpără SMS-uri în plus".

Shown at 1280 (desktop), 820 (tablet portrait) and 390 (phone), in light and dark, built only from the system's components.

**Resurse** — camere, echipamente sau spații pe care le rezervă clienții — have their own section and tab; they are the columns of the calendar's resource view. The late-cancellation fee is a toggle: off, clients read "Clienții pot anula online cu cel puțin 24 de ore înainte."; on, "Anulare gratuită cu cel puțin 24 de ore înainte. După aceea se reține {suma}."

After the product review: a fixed bar "Ai schimbări nesalvate" with "Salvează schimbările" appears as soon as something changes; the SMS reminder shows its estimate ("Aproximativ 420 de SMS-uri pe lună, la programul de acum."); new businesses start with the 24-hour SMS reminder on.
