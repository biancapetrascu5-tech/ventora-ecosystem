# HubAdminTeam

Business administration for the owner — team and invitations: members with role, apps and status; pending invitations with sent and expiry dates, resend and cancel.

Shown at 1280 (desktop), 820 (tablet portrait) and 390 (phone), in light and dark, built only from the system's components.

After the product review: the seats in use ("4 din 10 angajați") sit next to "Invită o persoană"; at the limit the button opens UpgradeDialog; expired invitations show "Retrimite" in the row and disappear from the list after 30 days (they stay in the audit log); "Oprește accesul" / "Redă accesul" in the row menu.
