# HubSetupRequest

Hub · Abonament și consum: what the owner sees at each step of a request with implementation (lot 3.8, proposed, S121). Keys `billing.setup.*` in `i18n/commercial.json`, 6 languages. The team's side is FounderSetupRequests.

A card "Implementare {app}" with the status, what happens now and the steps. Peach only when it's the owner's turn: the offer (implementation, monthly maintenance, estimated start; accept or decline) and "De plătit" (pay the implementation). Before the app is live, a line says its subscription starts on the day it goes live, not when the implementation is paid. Live: the date it started, the monthly maintenance, the next payment, "Deschide {app}". Cancelled (before payment) and refunded (after it) give the reason and, for a refund, the card.

Shown at 1280 and 390, light and dark, with a German and Italian test.
