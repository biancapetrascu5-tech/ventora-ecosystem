# HubInvite

Accepting an invitation and setting the password, on AuthLayout with `wide` (S63): who invited you, the business and your role, the apps you will get, live password requirements (lime when met), terms, "Acceptă invitația". The phone/dark frame shows an expired invitation with "Cere o invitație nouă".

Shown at 1280 (desktop), 820 (tablet portrait) and 390 (phone), in light and dark, built only from the system's components.

After the product review: the e-mail is shown as text (the account is made on the invited address); one password field with "Arată parola" (no repeat field); the rules are announced as they tick. After accepting: a role with one app opens that app; a role with several apps lands in the launcher the first time.

**Direction B (V17).** The invitation uses the sign-in's brand panel (logo, "Afacerea ta. Într-un singur loc.", the chevrons, "© 2026 Ventora Systems"); the form sits on the sheet, without a card. On phones the panel becomes a band with the logo and language, and the form rises over it.
