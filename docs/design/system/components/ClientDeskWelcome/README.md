# ClientDeskWelcome

ClientDesk — the first visit (S140). Romanian only for now; keys `onboarding.cd.*` in `i18n/onboarding.json`.

**What is different** (two cards): in EasyBook, salon clients come on their own — booking, coming, coming back; in ClientDesk you follow what a booking doesn't solve — an offer for a wedding, an event, a partnership with a company.

**Three steps** ("1 din 3 pași"): the first lead, the first pipeline (from a template: Evenimente, Mirese, Colaborări cu firme), the link with EasyBook — already on ("412 clienți din EasyBook apar ca contacte"), showing what is shared (name, phone, e-mail, visits, last booking) and what never reaches ClientDesk (behaviour note, blocked clients, health information). "Vezi cu date de exemplu" on the side; example deals carry the "Date de exemplu" band.

Shown at 1280, 820 and 390, light and dark.

**DE DECIS (S140, first start).**
- **Where the guide lives.** Recommendation: on EasyBook's "Azi" (drawn) and as one line in the Hub launcher ("Configurare EasyBook · 3 din 7"), until done or hidden.
- **Required steps.** Recommendation: only the first three (details and hours, services, team) block online booking; the rest are recommended; the deposit stays optional.
- **Service templates.** Recommendation: five business types at launch (drawn), with durations and prices as a starting point, not market data.
- **Import from another booking app.** Recommendation: recognise the export files of the 2–3 apps most used by the first salons; anything else through column matching.
- **Future bookings at import.** Recommendation: imported as "Importată", without messages to clients until the salon confirms them.
- **Example data.** Recommendation: a separate business "Salon Demo" (drawn), deleted automatically after 30 days if forgotten, never mixed with real data.
- **Tours.** Recommendation: at most 3 steps, shown once per screen and person, always skippable; no tours on phones for complex screens.
- **The pilot band.** Recommendation: on for the test salons until the end of the pilot, switched off from Founder; feedback goes to Founder › Suport as a request.
- **ClientDesk's first visit.** Recommendation: shown once per business; the EasyBook link is on by default when both apps are active.
