# HubChooseBusiness

Choosing the business for people who belong to several: each row shows the business, your role there, the number of apps and when you last used it. No "Adaugă afacere" — a missing business means asking its owner for an invitation.

Shown at 1280 (desktop), 820 (tablet portrait) and 390 (phone), in light and dark, built only from the system's components.

After the product review: the last business is first and focused (Enter opens it); a search appears above 6 businesses; each row's accessible name says name, role and when it was last opened.

**Suspended business (S112).** A suspended business stays in the list, with its role and the neutral pill "Acces oprit" (lock) in place of the chevron; it is not a button and cannot be opened. Under the list: „O afacere cu «Acces oprit» nu se poate deschide acum. Pentru detalii, scrie-ne la support@ventorasystems.com.” "Deschide direct ultima afacere" never opens a suspended business. When it is the person's only business, HubBusinessSuspended replaces this screen. Keys in `i18n/business-access.json`.
