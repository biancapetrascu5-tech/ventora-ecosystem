# BookingDemo

EasyBook and ClientDesk — example data (S140). Romanian only for now; keys `onboarding.demo.*` in `i18n/onboarding.json`.

"Vezi cu date de exemplu" (from the guide, an empty "Azi" and "?") creates a separate business, "Salon Demo", next to the real one, in the business switcher: 120 invented clients, a month of bookings with 4 colleagues and 12 services, deals and leads in ClientDesk, payments and reports. Salon Demo sends no e-mail or SMS, takes no payments and doesn't count in the plan's limits.

**The band.** A peach band on every page of Salon Demo: "Date de exemplu · Salon Demo. Nimic de aici nu e real și nu pleacă la clienți." with "Șterge datele de exemplu" (ClientDeskWelcome shows it on ClientDesk's deals).

**Deleting.** One click and a Dialog: Salon Demo disappears with everything; Salon Aurora doesn't change; it can be made again any time.

Shown at 1280, 820 and 390, light and dark.
