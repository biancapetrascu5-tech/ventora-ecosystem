# FounderPacks

Catalogue of "Pachete în plus": price, periodicity, setup cost, plans where available, direct purchase or on request. The single source for every price shown in offers.

**S112.** The members limit reads "membri" when counted ("10 membri") and "Locuri în echipă" as a resource or pack ("5 locuri în echipă în plus"); "Angajați" is only for the business's staff inside the apps.

**Lei and euro (S113).** Each pack has a price in lei and a price in euro, typed in separately, and the setup cost in both. Monthly packs bought mid-period are charged pro rata in the first period. The edit dialog shows both prices and how it is paid; packs already bought keep their old price until renewal.
