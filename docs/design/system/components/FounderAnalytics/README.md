# FounderAnalytics

Founder — Analytics (S136, D-PX-2, 5.12b). In the first group of the Founder Spine, after "Prezentare". Romanian only for now; keys `founder.analytics.*` in `i18n/founder.json`.

**No tracking cookies.** A neutral Notice on every tab: measured on the server, per business and per app, totals only; groups under 5 businesses are not shown; you never see what one person did.

**Filters.** Period (30 days, quarter, 6 months, year), app, plan.

**Produsul.** Stats: active businesses (at least one action in the last 30 days), apps per business, retention at 3 months, activated businesses (a second member reached). Active and paying businesses per month (LineChart); apps turned on and EasyBook feature adoption (horizontal BarCharts); cohort retention by month of sign-up (DataTable, months 1–5).

**Activarea.** The activation funnel as a horizontal BarChart, then the median time to each step and the drop-off.

**Sloturile Growth.** Impressions, clicks (of impressions), conversions (of clicks), the "Nu acum" rate; per offer: slot, impressions, clicks, conversions. A figure from a group under 5 businesses shows "Sub 5 afaceri", with a note.

**States.** No data yet.

Shown at 1280, 820 and 390, light and dark.
