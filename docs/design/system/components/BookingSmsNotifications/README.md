# BookingSmsNotifications

The operational notifications for the business phone (SPEC_ECRANE_SMS §7): in the bell's panel (Hub and every app) and by e-mail.

**Panel (S2).** NotificationsPanel now groups by `category`: "Despre cont și funcționare" on top, then "Activitate". The SMS items: "Telefonul afacerii e deconectat" ("6 reamintiri trebuie să plece până la 18:00. Verifică telefonul.") and "Mesajele nu pleacă" ("3 mesaje la rând nu au plecat de pe Telefon recepție."), and when the phone is back, "Telefonul afacerii s-a conectat din nou". Opening one goes to Settings · Trimitere SMS (or the message list). Who gets them: owner, admin and the EasyBook manager (S44).

**E-mail (S3).** One operational template for the whole ecosystem — team invitation, password reset, SMS alerts (no sign-in codes by e-mail: two-step sign-in uses an authenticator app) — in the 6 languages, light only, sent through Resend from ventora-hub.com, every one from Ventora <noreply@ventora-hub.com>. Parts: inbox line (sender, subject, preheader), the Ventora logo, eyebrow (business · app), title, the sentence that says what happened, "Ce să verifici" as a numbered list, one primary button, what happens next, and a footer with why you get it and the company. On phones the button takes the full width. Built for e-mail clients: one column, 600px, no dark mode.

**The two SMS e-mails.** "Telefonul afacerii e deconectat · Salon Aurora" (once per disconnection, after 15 minutes, only with messages waiting; at most 3 a day per business) and "Mesajele nu pleacă de pe telefonul afacerii · Salon Aurora" (after 3 messages in a row did not leave; at most once a day). Both go to Settings with one button.

**E-mail preferences (Contul tău · Notificări).** Each person can turn off the e-mail copy, except the owner: the toggle is on and locked, with "Proprietarul primește mereu aceste e-mailuri, ca să afle cineva sigur când reamintirile nu pleacă." The in-app notifications can't be turned off. Recipients: owner, admins and the EasyBook manager.

Shown with the panel open at 1280, 820 and 390, the panel states in light and dark, the e-mails (the two SMS ones, the team invitation — "Invitația e valabilă până pe {data}.", because a member's invitation lasts 7 days and the owner's 30 — and the password reset — "Linkul e valabil o oră." (S55), desktop and phone), the e-mail preferences for owner and admin, and a German and Italian test of the e-mail and the panel.

**"Parola contului tău a fost schimbată" (S78, S82, S83).** The same template, from Ventora <noreply@ventora-hub.com>, for every password change, in two variants: from "Ai uitat parola?" ("… Te-am scos din cont pe toate dispozitivele.") and from Contul tău · Securitate ("… Te-am scos din cont pe celelalte dispozitive."). The time is in the fixed zone of the other e-mails, with its abbreviation ("10:32 EET"). "Nu ai fost tu?" names "Ai uitat parola?" and support@ventorasystems.com as text only; the one button is "Scrie-ne la suport" (an e-mail to support). No reset link, no password. It can't be turned off: in Contul tău · Notificări it shows only as an informational row ("E-mailuri de securitate…"), without a toggle (S83). Shown in both variants, on phone, and in German and Italian; texts from `i18n/password-reset.json` (`auth.reset.email.*`).

**E-6 "Echipa Ventora are acces de suport la {businessName}" (S97).** To the owners when someone from the Ventora team opens a support session (HubSupportSession). Romanian only; same template, from Ventora <noreply@ventora-hub.com>; subject = title; preheader = the intro; eyebrow {businessName}; "Bună, {name}," (or "Bună," without a name); who opened it and for which request; what the team can and can't do; "Motivul: {reason}"; "Sesiunea se închide automat pe {expiresAt}." (written "28 octombrie, la 18:30 EET": fixed zone with abbreviation); how to close it from the banner; in bold "Dacă nu ai cerut ajutor, închide sesiunea și scrie-ne la support@ventorasystems.com."; signature "Echipa Ventora". No button. Footer: "Primești acest e-mail pentru că ești proprietarul afacerii {businessName} în Ventora." Keys as in code: `email.supportSession.*` (subject, intro, scope, until, close, notRequested), plus `reason` and `footer` (S99). Shown with a name (600) and without a name on a phone (390). The template now also takes a greeting, a bold paragraph and a signature.
